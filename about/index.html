About

Matei Alexandru Anghel

I am a software engineer with six years in high-performance systems, and the founder of Broadside Technology Corp. I architect systems end to end, from lock-free engine cores and allocators to compilers and data pipelines, and I build them at speed with frontier coding agents.

I have a tremendous ongoing interest in context engineering, and increasingly in long-living agentic networks such as those described in the AI Village. The central question of preserving human intent through long-running workflows, with hundreds or thousands of AI agents, is currently my main subject of study.

I studied computer science at John Abbott College and Concordia University, and left to build full time. I am Canadian, fluent in English and French, and open to remote work or relocation.

I am a perfectionist.
